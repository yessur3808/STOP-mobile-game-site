ONE SMALL TAP. ONE GIANT GLIZZY.

Glizzy Mission

A floppy hot dog. A questionable space mission. An offline timing game for iPhone and iPad.

How to play

Tap Launch mission, then tap once when the aim lines up with the opening. Clear five obstacles to finish a delivery. Mustard pickups are optional: they count only when you clear the obstacle. Pick Bread Insurance, Mustard Magnet, or Slow Sauce between deliveries.

Outfits and progress

Open the wardrobe from the home screen or pause menu. Seven outfits are available from the start; delivery stars, achievements, and level milestones unlock twenty more. Outfits do not change the collision area. Progress and settings are saved on your device. Deleting the app may remove them; there is no separate cloud account.

Sound and screen changes

Music, sound effects, and haptics have separate controls. Music starts after interaction. If you rotate, resize, or leave the app during a run, tap Resume when ready. The game supports portrait and landscape layouts. Reduce Motion limits decorative animation.

Found a weird wobble?

Email yaser.developer.ios@icloud.com. Include your device, iOS version, app version, what happened, and steps to repeat it. The in-game help screen can copy a feedback template. Screenshots are welcome; please omit sensitive information.
