Privacy policy

Glizzy Mission · Effective 20 November 2026

An offline game

Glizzy Mission does not collect or transmit personal data through the game. It has no accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics service, in-app purchases, or network-backed gameplay.

What stays on your device

Your best score, preferences for music, effects and haptics, earned stars, unlocks, and selected outfit are stored in app-specific local preferences. The game does not upload these values. Deleting the app removes local data, subject to operating-system backup and restore behavior.

Support you choose to send

The feedback template is copied only when you tap the copy button. Nothing is sent automatically. If you email support, your email address and any details you choose to provide are used to respond and investigate the issue. Please avoid sending sensitive information. To request deletion of support correspondence, contact the address below.

Apple services

Apple may process App Store diagnostics according to your device settings and Apple's policies. If you use a TestFlight version, Apple handles TestFlight diagnostics and tester-submitted feedback under its TestFlight terms. The game does not include a third-party analytics SDK.

This support website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process technical request information such as IP addresses for delivery and security under GitHub's privacy statement. These pages contain no advertising, analytics scripts, or tracking cookies.
