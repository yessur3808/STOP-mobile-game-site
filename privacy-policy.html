Pocket Railway

Privacy Policy · Updated 10 November 2026

Overview

STOP Game built Pocket Railway as a lightweight timing game. The game works offline, requires no account to play offline, and does not sell personal information.

Data stored on your device

Pocket Railway saves your best scores, top-five score history and timestamps, settings, tutorial status, and unlocked stage locally on your device. Your private Friends comparison list stores selected player identifiers and usernames only on this device. It is not uploaded as a social graph and does not access your contacts. You can remove friends individually, and deleting your online profile clears the list. Downloaded public leaderboard snapshots and their last-updated timestamps are also saved locally for offline viewing. Deleting your online profile clears these snapshots. Removing the app or resetting its data may erase this information.

Optional analytics

Anonymous Analytics is off by default. If you enable it in Settings, Firebase Analytics may process gameplay events, app interactions, approximate location derived from network information, device information, and an app-instance identifier to help improve difficulty and reliability. Advertising identifiers and ad-personalization signals are disabled. You can turn analytics off at any time.

Optional online leaderboard profiles

If you choose Join Leaderboards, Pocket Railway creates a device-based Firebase Authentication account and stores your chosen username, home country or region, device platform (iOS or Android), all-time and current-week best ranked scores, independent best perfect streaks, and server timestamps in Google Firebase. Your username, country or region, platform, score and streak are visible to other players. Your chosen country is not determined using GPS. A random account identifier associates your profile and scores with your account.

This information is used for leaderboards and profile management, not advertising. Online rankings are separate from the optional analytics setting. Gameplay remains available without joining, and network access is only needed for online features. Scores waiting for upload are stored locally until a connection is available.

Unlinked profiles belong to this installation. Removing app data may prevent you from accessing them again. If you choose to link Game Center or Google Play Games, Firebase associates that gaming identity with your profile so you can restore your online profile and records using the same gaming account on the same platform. Local game history and friends are not cloud-synced. Deleting a linked profile may require confirming the gaming account. To remove it first, open World Journey → Leaderboards → Profile → Delete Online Profile. This removes the public ranking, username reservation, profile and authentication account. It does not erase local gameplay records. We retain online profile data until you request deletion. Google's service retention and security practices also apply. For help deleting a profile you can no longer access, contact curlysloth510@gmail.com with your username. We may request information to verify that you control the profile. Please do not send passwords or sign-in codes.

Choose a nickname rather than your real name. To report an inappropriate username or ranking, contact the support address with the username and a screenshot.

Gaming and sharing services

If you use Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, those services handle sign-in under their own privacy policies. Pocket Railway may read your public player name to place it on a score card. Score cards and optional silent run-replay videos are generated on your device. The last six seconds of gameplay are kept temporarily in memory for replay sharing and are discarded on a new run or when the app closes. Replays are not uploaded to our servers. Media are generated on your device and shared only when you choose a destination in the operating system share sheet. Pocket Railway does not upload them to its own server.

Device features

Sound and haptics are processed on your device. Pocket Railway does not request contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or photo-library access for normal gameplay.

Ads and purchases

This version contains no advertising SDK, in-app purchases, or paid subscriptions. This policy will be updated before either is introduced.

Children

Pocket Railway is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children. Online profiles ask for a nickname and home country or region, not a real name or contact details. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children where parental permission is required. Contact us if a child has submitted personal information so we can remove it.

Your choices

You can delete your optional online profile from Leaderboards, disable analytics, music, sound, and haptics in Settings, reset the best score, and manage gaming sign-in and sharing through your device and Apple or Google account settings.
