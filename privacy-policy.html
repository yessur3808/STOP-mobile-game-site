STOP

Privacy Policy · Effective 3 September 2026

Overview

STOP Game built STOP as a lightweight timing game. The game works offline, requires no STOP account, and does not sell personal information.

Data stored on your device

STOP saves your best scores, top-five score history and timestamps, settings, tutorial status, and unlocked stage locally on your device. Removing the app or resetting its data may erase this information.

Optional analytics

Anonymous Analytics is off by default. If you enable it in Settings, Firebase Analytics may process gameplay events, app interactions, approximate location derived from network information, device information, and an app-instance identifier to help improve difficulty and reliability. Advertising identifiers and ad-personalization signals are disabled. You can turn analytics off at any time.

Gaming and sharing services

If you use Apple Game Center or Google Play Games, those services handle sign-in under their own privacy policies. STOP may read your public player name to place it on a score card. Score cards are generated on your device and shared only when you choose a destination in the operating system share sheet. STOP does not upload them to its own server.

Device features

Sound and haptics are processed on your device. STOP does not request contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, or photo-library access for normal gameplay.

Ads and purchases

This version contains no advertising SDK, in-app purchases, or paid subscriptions. This policy will be updated before either is introduced.

Children

STOP is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children. We do not knowingly request names, email addresses, or other direct contact information from players.

Your choices

You can disable analytics, sound, and haptics in Settings, reset the best score, and manage gaming sign-in and sharing through your device and Apple or Google account settings.
