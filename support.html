Pocket Railway

Help for the one-tap train challenge on iPhone and Android.

How to play

Tap anywhere while the center of the train is inside the station target. GOOD, GREAT, and PERFECT all keep the run alive. A miss or an empty timer ends the run.

Scores and settings

Scores, the top-five history, unlocked routes, and settings are stored on your device. Reinstalling the app or clearing app data may remove them. Use Settings in the app to control sound, haptics, analytics, and score history.

Game Center and Play Games

Platform sign-in is optional. Where profile linking is available, use Leaderboards → Profile to link your gaming account and recover your online profile and records on another device of the same platform. Local Top 5 history and friends stay on your device. Platform sign-in can also personalize share cards. The game works without it. You can manage platform sign-in through your Apple or Google account settings.

Online profiles and rankings

Joining the leaderboards is optional. Choose a nickname and home country. Only new eligible runs after joining are posted. Offline rankings show saved results with their last update time. Local game progress does not depend on an online account.

Delete a profile or report a username

Open Leaderboards → Profile → Delete Online Profile to remove your ranking, username reservation, profile and authentication account. A linked gaming account may need to be confirmed first. Local game progress is kept. For deletion assistance or to report an inappropriate username, email curlysloth510@gmail.com with the player's username and a short explanation. Please never send passwords or sign-in codes.

Share cards

Pocket Railway creates share images and short animated score cards on your device. The system share sheet decides which installed apps can receive them. If a destination does not accept an animation, try the still-image option.

Troubleshooting

If sound is missing, check the in-game Sound toggle and your device volume. If haptics are missing, check the Haptics toggle and system settings. After returning from another app, tap once to resume a paused run.

Contact

Email curlysloth510@gmail.com. Include your device model, operating-system version, app version, and a short description of what happened. Please do not send passwords or payment information.
